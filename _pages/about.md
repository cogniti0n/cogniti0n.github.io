I am an undergradute student at the department of [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://ece.snu.ac.kr/) at [Seoul National University](https://en.snu.ac.kr/). My research interests lie broadly in foundations of deep learning and reinforcement learning.

I am currently studying under the supervision of Prof. [Min-hwan Oh](https://minoh.io/). My current research topics include decision making under uncertainty and optimization for machine learning. I previously studied integration of dynamical neuronal models and open quantum systems with network science under the supervision of Prof. [Sunkyu Yu](https://waves.snu.ac.kr/).

I hope to connect adjacent fields of interest, including statistical learning theory, optimization, cognitive science, and network science to construct a dynamical theory of intelligence.
